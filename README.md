# 📊 Online Retail Performance Analysis Dashboard

## 📝 Project Overview

This project presents a dashboard analysis of an online retail store's sales data. As consultants, our objective was to review historical data (primarily from 2011) to understand key revenue drivers, identify factors behind sales peaks, explore potential markets for expansion, and suggest strategies for increasing customer purchase frequency.

The insights derived are intended to inform the management team's strategic planning for operational efficiency, effective marketing, and informed decision-making regarding business expansion.

## 🏢 Business Context

As a consultant hired by the online retail store, I was tasked with reviewing their data to understand the major contributing factors to their revenue. The management team is keen to leverage these insights for strategic planning in the coming year, focusing on both operational efficiency and effective marketing strategies. Furthermore, with plans for business expansion, understanding current strengths and high-performing areas is crucial for informed decision-making.

## 🖥️ Dashboard Sections & Key Insights

The analysis is presented across three main dashboard views:

### 1. 📈 Behind the Sales Peak

*   **Focus:** Analyzing the factors contributing to the significant sales increase observed in the end-year period of 2011.
*   **💡 Key Findings:**
    *   The end-year period in 2011 (specifically September - November) generated the most sales revenue.
    *   The primary drivers of this peak were the **increase in the frequency and number of customers** (both new and returning) and the **repetition of orders**.
    *   Analysis indicates that **price and order size were *not* significant contributors** to this specific sales surge.
*   **👉 Recommendation:** Concentrate promotional efforts during the end-year period (Sept-Nov) to capitalize on this trend of increased customer activity and order repetition.

### 2. 🌍 Market Expansion

*   **Focus:** Identifying promising international markets for expansion and highlighting top-performing products to potentially promote.
*   **💡 Key Findings:**
    *   Besides the UK, **Netherlands, EIRE (Ireland), Germany, France, and Australia** stand out as potential markets for expansion, showing the largest customer revenue generation.
    *   These markets have established customer bases with concrete revenue flow.
    *   **🔥 Hot products** with high unit sales volume include:
        *   Rabbit Night Light (StockCode: 23084)
        *   Mini Paint Set Vintage (StockCode: 22492)
        *   Retrospot Cake Case (StockCode: 21212)
*   **👉 Recommendation:** Consider these countries for market expansion efforts. Promote top-performing products identified in these potential new markets.

### 3. 🔄 Increase Customer's Frequency

*   **Focus:** Understanding customer segmentation and behavior to devise strategies for increasing repeat purchases, especially among key customer segments.
*   **💡 Key Findings:**
    *   Revenue generation is significantly skewed, with a subset of customers contributing disproportionately (**Pareto principle likely applies**).
    *   The RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis reveals that key customers often exhibit **high recency but low frequency**. This presents a clear opportunity.
    *   There are opportunities to gain more by improving engagement and retention within key customer groups.
    *   **🔥 Hot products** suggested for marketing campaigns aimed at promoting repeat orders include:
        *   Popcorn Holder
        *   Restropot
        *   WW2 Gliders Designs
*   **👉 Recommendation:** Implement targeted marketing campaigns focusing on key, high-recency but low-frequency customers. Promote relevant "hot products" to encourage repeat purchases and build loyalty. Build rapport and close reporting gaps for these valuable customers.
